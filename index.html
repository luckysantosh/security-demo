Phishing warning signs

  • Unexpected request: You did not expect the login message or link.
  • Suspicious address: The website address does not match the legitimate service.
  • Urgency: The message pressures you to act immediately.
  • Password request: A page unexpectedly asks for sensitive information.
  • Unusual message: Spelling, wording, or formatting may look inconsistent.
Safe habit:
When in doubt, open the official service directly instead of following an unexpected login link.
