Phishing red flags to check:
  • Check the website address carefully.
  • Be cautious with unexpected login requests.
  • Watch for urgency or threats.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • When unsure, use the service's official app or website.

URL Awareness Exercise

Before entering credentials, identify the actual domain in the address.

https://example.com/account/login

In this example, example.com is the domain. The rest is the path.

Don't assume a familiar-looking page is legitimate just because it uses HTTPS or looks professional.
